# Group sense-making (full mode, several people)

Use this when the situation is contested or several people must agree on the domain.
Skip it when one person asks a direct question.

## Why a group method

People disagree about the domain because they hold different experience.
An expert often sees "Complicated" where a newcomer sees "Complex".
Settle the disagreement with evidence, not with rank.

## Method: contextualise, then place

1. **Write events.** Each person writes 5 to 10 short facts or events about the situation
   on separate cards. Use concrete wording ("Support tickets doubled in March").
2. **Cluster.** Group the cards by theme. Name each cluster. A cluster is a candidate **piece**.
3. **Place.** For each cluster, answer the four questions in `classification.md`.
   Each person answers alone first. Then compare.
4. **Read the spread.** If people split between two domains, that is a finding.
   Write it as the `second_reading` and its `tipping_condition`.
5. **Decide the approach** for each piece. Complex pieces get probes. Do not plan them.

## Rules

- Answer privately first. Group talk anchors on the first speaker.
- Treat disagreement on Complicated versus Complex as a sign of **Complex**.
- Record the minority view in `second_reading`.
- Timebox the session. A sense-making session that lasts longer than the probes it leads to has failed.

## Output

Fill one assessment JSON with `mode: "full"`. Run the scripts in SKILL.md.
Share the rendered report. Do not share the cards.
